Engaged Research Planning for Impact

By: Campus Engage and Irish Universities Association

Format: External Resource
Time: 13 min
Stage: Developing an Idea
Pathway: Academic Scholarship, Community Engagement
planningengaged researchfundingstrategy

A clear and concise framework for understanding research impact and how you can start planning for it. Informed by recent literature and international consultation.

Who it is for

Researchers at the early stages of planning a project or drafting a funding application.

What you will do

A guide informed by academic literature and consultation with researchers, policymakers, and funding agency personnel. Provides inspiration and guidance for planning engaged research and drafting funding applications.

Outcomes

A clearer understanding of research impact and a starting framework for your impact plan.
