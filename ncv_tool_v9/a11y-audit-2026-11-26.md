# Accessibility audit of V9.3 (2026-11-26)

Prem asked: "run the accessibility check on V9.3". Tool: `ncv_tool_v9/a11y-audit.js` (new, reusable). It drives `narrative-cv-prototype-v9.html` in headless Chrome with the mock CV (`ncv_tool_v8/mock/mock-narrative-cv-draft.json`) and needs a local axe-core (`AXE=/path/to/axe.min.js`).

```bash
NCV_CDP_PORT=9370 node ncv_tool_v9/a11y-audit.js
```

## What was checked

18 states, each at 1280 px and at 320 px (a phone, touch on): Start (new, with a draft, after Clear draft), Set up your profile, Contributions (and with the evidence view open), the Change panel, Mentorship (and the Undo after Remove person), Personal statement, the five Review tabs, Review's evidence editor (the Column choice) and its Undo, and Export.

- **axe-core 4.11.4**, every WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rule (contrast included).
- **Accessibility tree** (Chrome's own): every button, link, field, tab, radio and checkbox has a name.
- **Headings**: no level skipped; **ids**: none repeated.
- **Targets**: 24 px on every screen (WCAG 2.5.8), 44 px on a phone (D-14). Links inside a sentence are exempt, as WCAG allows.
- **Reflow**: no sideways scroll of the page at 320 px (WCAG 1.4.10).
- **Keyboard walk** at 1280 px: Tab through every control on each state; focus never lands on a hidden element, and every stop shows a focus ring.
- **Keyboard runs of the V9.2 and V9.3 controls**: the Column choice (arrow keys), Save, Escape, Remove, Undo, Export's Edit, Clear draft and its Undo, Undecided with Space.

## Found and fixed

| Finding | Where | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| axe `scrollable-region-focusable`: the "Where they are now" chart scrolls sideways at 320 px but could not be reached by keyboard | Review, Mentorship, every tab at 320 px | The chart box takes focus when it scrolls (role group, named by its caption "Where they are now"), so arrow keys scroll it; when it fits it is not a tab stop |
| Link-style buttons that stand alone were 15 to 23 px tall (WCAG 2.5.8 asks 24) | ← Continue in the rail; Change (evidence kind); Hide or show statement; See personal statement; Edit on Contributions; the checklist links | 24 px minimum on every screen |
| Under 44 px on a phone (D-14) | Review tabs (40), area marks (32), + Add in Add evidence (28), See a finished example (43), the draft name field (35), Clear draft on the start card (32), the title link (26), short link-style buttons such as Undo, Edit, Hide (24 to 43 wide) | 44 px on a phone or touch screen |
| A regression found by the re-run: the new 24 px rule overrode the phone's 44 px rule for link-style buttons | Every step at 320 px | The phone rule restated after it |

After the fixes: axe-core finds **no WCAG failure** in any state at either width; **no control without a name**; **no heading skips or repeated ids**; **no sideways scroll** at 320 px; every Tab stop shows focus; all 8 keyboard runs pass. Three checks were added to `ncv_tool_v9/test-v9.js` (669 tests pass).

## Left open

- **The word bar on "Edit what already changed" at 320 px**: its narrowest parts are 28 to 37 px wide (44 px tall). That meets WCAG 2.5.8 (24 px) and misses D-14's 44 px. The parts are sized by word count, so widening them would misstate the shares; a fix needs a design choice (for example, a list of the parts under the bar on a phone).
- **The Tab walk passes `<body>` once per page**: this is the browser's wrap from the last control back to "Skip to main content", not a gap in the page.

## Not covered

- No run with a real screen reader (VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS); names, roles, states and live-region text were read from Chrome's accessibility tree and the page's live region.
- Chrome only (Safari and Firefox not run).
- 200% zoom was not run separately; the 320 px reflow check covers WCAG 1.4.10. Reduced motion is covered by the existing test suite.
- Colour contrast is axe-core's computation on the rendered states; text over images or gradients would need a manual look (the tool has none).
