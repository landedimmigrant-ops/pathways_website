Part colours without the edge stripe

A before-and-after of every place the Narrative CV tool draws a part's colour as a stripe on one edge of a rounded shape. All content here is invented. Notes, evidence and the full list: accent-review-2026-11-26.md.

Recommended: a part's colour is shown as a part box (the part's pale tint as the ground, a 1px line of the part's colour all round, and 2px corners) and never as a stripe on one edge. The part's name stays in text.

  • No colour on one edge only. In Concordia's shared stylesheet a one-sided line marks only the current page in a menu; on the Pathways pages the one exception is the landing page's pathway list, and there the line is straight.
  • Corners of 2px on anything that carries a part colour (Concordia's own corner size for cards and buttons).
  • Gold uses a darker line (#8a6200) so the line reaches 3:1 on white and on the gold tint.
