# Tier 3 — External Resources (Not for Beta)

Resources excluded from beta launch due to time commitment, scope, or internal-use intent.

## 1. Public Engagement Evaluation Toolkit

- **Author:** Queen Mary University of London
- **Purpose:** A practical toolkit for designing and implementing effective evaluation strategies for public and community-engaged research. Offers step-by-step guidance, templates, and real examples.
- **Value Proposition:** Evaluate your engagement activities with confidence. Provides practical advice, worksheets, and real-world examples to plan and conduct high-quality evaluations.
- **Pathway Tags:** Communications, Community Engagement
- **Stage:** Developing an Idea, Finishing a Project
- **Format:** Deep Dive Resource
- **Time:** 45 minutes
- **Link:** https://www.qmul.ac.uk/publicengagement/support/guides/evaluation-toolkit/
- **Reason for exclusion:** Too deep/heavy for beta launch. Consider post-launch.

## 2. Research Impact Workshops (NWO)

- **Author:** NWO (Dutch Research Council)
- **Purpose:** Self-guided online workshops providing a general overview of research impact and how to plan for it, covering both scientific and societal impact.
- **Value Proposition:** A deep dive into research impact planning through self-guided online workshops.
- **Pathway Tags:** Academic Scholarship
- **Stage:** Developing an Idea
- **Format:** Deep Dive Resource
- **Time:** 6 hours (across three workshops)
- **Link:** https://impact.nwo.nl/en
- **Reason for exclusion:** 6-hour commitment far exceeds beta resource scope.
