## Hiccups

### H1. NEW — `my <noun>` counts as an ownership marker, so "We partnered… our findings" passes when "My research…" is nearby — `Medium / lint regression (V5 B-18)`
The V4 run caught this trap; V5 misses it. Xray paragraph 2 (*I am an assistant professor… My
research focuses… We partnered with community kitchens and our findings were shared widely… My
work has been published…*) reads "✓ Team voice and your own role, both visible". Probes on an
empty field: `We partnered with community kitchens and our findings were shared widely. My
research focuses on food environments.` → strength; the same two sentences with `I am an
assistant professor.` instead of the *My research* sentence → "⚠ 'we' or 'our' appears 2 times
with no 'I …' nearby". So the culprit is the beta's broadening of the individual-marker test to
any `my <word>` — *my research* / *my work* are topic statements, not claims of role. Fix sketch:
restrict `my` to people/role nouns (group, lab, team, students, collaborators, trainees, role,
part, contribution, responsibility) — keep the verb broadening, which is doing its job (see
closures below).

### H2. NEW — "Choose any that apply" collapses after the first pick — `Low-Medium / UX (V5 B-6)`
Help text: *Pick more than one if more than one is true — most people are more than one.* After
one chip the row is replaced by "How your work happens: Community-engaged · Change"; the second
mode needs *Change* → chip. Multi-select that hides itself after one choice reads as
single-select. Fix sketch: keep the row open while the question has focus / until the researcher
scrolls past it, or collapse only when 2+ are chosen or after an explicit *Done*.

### H3. NEW — Read-back marks don't appear in Structural-read cards — `Low / consistency (V5 B-16)`
Every paragraph card that flagged something has `marks: []`; the same flags on a Contributions
field show the text with the words marked. The pasted-draft path is where "which words triggered
this" is most useful. Fix sketch: call `renderReadback` from `analyzeDraft`'s card renderer.

### H4. NEW — "At least one…" coverage rows say "✓ done" and "missing on 3" at once — `Low / copy-logic (V5 B-34)`
"At least one full contribution — headline, what you did, what already changed — missing on 3.
Community data governance… ✓ done" and the evidence row likewise. Correct for the "Every
contribution has a role line — missing on 3 … not yet" row. Fix sketch: only append the missing
list when the row is unmet, or when the row is an "every" row.

### H5. NEW — Prestige flag names one term and the metrics' own numbers still count as specificity — `Low / lint nuance (residual of nadia-2026-08-10 H3)`
Xray paragraph 2: "⚠ 'h-index' — reviewers are told to disregard…" alongside "✓ 2 specific
numbers" (14.2 and 11 — the very numbers the flag says to cut). *high-impact* matched silently
(not named); `IF 14.2` isn't matched at all. Fix sketch: list every matched term; exclude numbers
within ~3 tokens of a prestige term from the count; add `\bIF\s*\d` to the pattern.
