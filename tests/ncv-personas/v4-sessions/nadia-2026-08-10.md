**Review.** The coverage list caught my stub contribution ("Each contribution has a role line — not done"), though it didn't say *which* one. ## Hiccups

### H1. NEW — Work-mode "(choose any)" is single-shot, then unreachable — `Medium-High / UX`
The just-in-time work-mode row renders only while `lens.workMode` is empty (`renderContributions`, narrative-cv-prototype-v4.html:748). The first chip click re-renders the stage, the condition goes false, and the row disappears — "(choose any)" is effectively "choose one." No other surface edits work mode: Setup has no work-mode chips, and "Edit context" leads there. Evidence: after clicking Community-engaged, `Team-based` button no longer exists in the DOM; lens pills show only "Community-engaged"; final state `workMode: ["community"]` for a persona who is community-engaged *and* team-based. Consequences: translational-prompt gating and outcome-prompt variants run on an incomplete profile. Fix sketch: keep the row visible with current selections (drop the emptiness condition), and/or add work-mode chips to Setup.

### H2. NEW — "I am a assistant professor" — anchor article breaks on vowel roles — `Low / polish, first line of the CV`
The PS anchor hardcodes "I am a" (:1030, export :1172, print :1260). Any vowel-initial role (assistant/associate professor, epidemiologist, engineer) produces "a assistant…" in the UI line, the .txt export, and the print view — the very first sentence a reviewer reads. Fix sketch: `/^[aeiou]/i.test(role) ? "an" : "a"` at all three sites (or let the researcher edit the article).

### H3. NEW — Banned prestige metrics are *rewarded* as specificity — `Medium / lint gap (genre)`
"published in a high-impact journal (IF 14.2) and my h-index is 11" earned "✓ 2 specific numbers" in the structural read — the exact metrics the tri-agency reviewer guidelines ban as surrogates (ncv-genre-sources.md §1, cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/54339.html). "high-impact journal" itself was flagged by the vague list (good), but "IF 14.2" and "h-index is 11" drew *positive* credit, and the document-level "evidence references" coverage row was satisfied *by* the word "journal" inside that same banned phrase. Fix sketch: a JIF/h-index/prestige detector (`\bIF\s*\d`, `impact factor`, `h-index`, `top-tier|prestigious (venue|journal)`) that flags AND excludes those numbers from the specificity count.

### H4. NEW — Merged heading digit inflates the specificity count — `Low / lint accuracy`
Because single-newline blocks merge heading+body (KNOWN #9), the heading's ordinal ("2.") is counted as a "specific number" for the body paragraph — my pilot paragraph's only credited "number" was its own list index. Interaction bug between #9 and the number counter; fixing #9's segmentation also fixes this.

### H5. NEW — Coverage rows don't say which contribution failed — `Low / findability`
"Each contribution has a role line —" correctly went un-done (my title-only stub), but with three contributions I had to guess where. Fix sketch: append the failing contribution's number/title to the row label.

### H6. NEW (confirmation) — Passive-voice agency erasure passes silently — `Medium / lint gap (genre)`
"A community advisory board was established and a screening protocol was developed" — my role erased entirely — drew no reaction anywhere. Known analysis candidate (§4.1-B passive detector), now confirmed against a realistic draft: the sentence pattern the genre most warns against is invisible to every current check.

### H7. KNOWN (V4 #1) — "May 2024" flagged as hedging, twice, with real cost
Fired in the structural read (Paragraph 4) and again on my retyped stakes. As a deferring early-career user I *rewrote a truthful date* ("as of May 2024" → "until 2024") to satisfy the tool — the false positive cost accuracy, not just time. This will hit any draft with a May date.

### H8. KNOWN (V4 #2) — Spelled-out numbers invisible; forced style-vs-tool conflict
"three community organizations… seven years… six data collectors" → only the digit "12" counted ("✓ 1 specific number"). Outputs and Outcomes drew "no numbers" flags I cleared by *adding digits the style guide says to spell out* ("12-week pilot"). The deference tax: two rewrites purely to appease the counter. Also surfaced the design question already in the analysis: Impact (a hypothesis) demanding quantities reads wrong.

### H9. KNOWN (V4 #8/#9) — Markdown headers linted; single-newline blocks merged
`**Personal Statement**` and `**Research Contributions**` each became a "paragraph" flagged for having no numbers; both numbered contributions merged with their heading lines, so `isHeading` never fired. Exactly as documented; my draft is the realistic Word-ish paste the analysis predicted.

### H10. KNOWN (V4 #10, nuance) — Evidence detection satisfied by the wrong evidence
Coverage said "Evidence references ✓ detected" — but only because "high-impact journal" contains the literal word "journal." "cited 41 times" and the named venue *The Lancet Regional Health – Americas* still match nothing; the pure false-negative case couldn't be shown with this draft because the banned-metrics phrase happened to rescue it. The detector's pass criteria and the genre's banned list currently *overlap*.

### H11. KNOWN (V4 #12) — Role line nagged for numbers
"I led the audit design and analysis; community partners led…" → "⚠ No numbers, dates, or quantities." A one-line role statement has no business carrying quantities.

### H12. KNOWN (U1) — Mentorship and Personal statement have zero feedback
Confirmed from the researcher's seat, with a planted control: my deliberately thin standing line ("some early adoption") — precisely the vague-signals pattern the contribution lint catches — passed in silence. The support gradient is inverted: most coaching on the safest fields, none on the highest-stakes prose.

### H13. KNOWN (U6) — The retype wall between Structural read and Contributions
The bridge note ("rebuild the weakest contribution…") pointed me the right way, but everything I'd pasted had to be retyped by hand in the builder. My draft was visible in one tab of the tool and unreachable in the next. This was the single largest friction of the session (~half the contribution-stage time was re-entry of existing text).

### H14. KNOWN (migration-review #41) — Title-only stub exports as a numbered contribution
Contribution 3 (title, nothing else) exports as a numbered item carrying four `[gap]` lines. Better than a bare header, but a reviewer-facing draft shouldn't number an empty claim; expected filter-before-numbering behavior.

### H15. NEW (setup) — "TCV vs CV-FRQ" never expanded — `Low / copy`
The agency chip row's only explanation is "Sets the format (TCV vs CV-FRQ)". First-time applicants (this persona) don't know either acronym; the July analysis noted it in prose but it was never ticketed. One parenthetical fixes it ("Tri-agency CV / Fonds de recherche du Québec CV").
