# Tier 1 — External Resources (Beta Launch)

Curated external tools integrated into the Explore section. These are immediate-use, short-commitment resources that map cleanly to existing pathways and stages.

---

## 1. Impact Narrative Tool

- **Author:** Collaboration between 5 organizations (Municipality of Delft, Erasmus Medical Center Rotterdam, Erasmus University Rotterdam, Netherlands Center for Youth Health, Healthy Start)
- **Purpose:** A digital tool to help researchers articulate the societal relevance and impact potential of their work through a structured narrative. Guides researchers in connecting research goals, activities, stakeholders, and expected outcomes into a coherent impact narrative.
- **Value Proposition:** Build a strong, compelling impact narrative for your research. This interactive tool helps you connect your project's rationale, stakeholders, activities, and expected contributions into a clear and persuasive story. Ideal for grant proposals and strategic planning.
- **Pathway Tags:** Communications
- **Stage:** Developing an Idea, Finishing a Project
- **Format:** Immediate Tool
- **Time:** 20 minutes
- **Link:** https://impactnarrative.nl/en/impact-narrative-tool/
- **Notes:** Strong fit — interactive, clear value for grant writing

---

## 2. Impact and Engagement Case Study Guidelines

- **Author:** Research Impact Canada
- **Purpose:** A structured set of guidelines to support researchers in crafting clear, evidence-informed case studies that highlight pathways to impact and engagement. Help researchers tell a compelling, credible story about research influence.
- **Value Proposition:** Tell the story of your research impact with clarity and confidence. These guidelines walk researchers through how to capture evidence, describe engagement activities, and present a compelling case study grounded in real-world outcomes.
- **Pathway Tags:** Community Engagement, Communications
- **Stage:** Developing an Idea, Finishing a Project
- **Format:** Immediate Tool (pages 10-17)
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Link:** https://researchimpact.ca/wp-content/uploads/RIC_Impact-and-Engagement-Case-Study-Guidelines-EN.pdf
- **Notes:** PDF resource — actionable subset from pages 10-17

---

## 3. Impact Indicators: Overview and Menu Selection

- **Author:** Erasmus University Rotterdam
- **Purpose:** A practical guide explaining what impact indicators are and how to write them, plus an inspirational menu of examples of outputs and outcomes that can be tailored to different research contexts.
- **Value Proposition:** How do you know if your research is making a difference? This tool helps you showcase how your work relates to society. Includes a guide on impact indicators and a broad list of examples to help identify and develop research outputs and outcomes indicators.
- **Pathway Tags:** Academic Scholarship, Impact Planning
- **Stage:** Developing an Idea, Finishing a Project
- **Format:** Immediate Tool
- **Time:** 30 minutes
- **Link:** https://www.eur.nl/en/research/research-services/societal-impact-evaluation/impact-toolbox/impact-indicators
- **Notes:** Strong fit — practical, planning focus

---

## 4. Research Impact Framework

- **Author:** Giovanna Lima and Sarah Bowman
- **Purpose:** A framework to help researchers understand and plan their impact across multiple domains — academic, societal, environmental, cultural, and policy-related contributions.
- **Value Proposition:** Explore and expand the potential range of your research impacts. Map your influence across multiple dimensions and identify new opportunities to plan strategically and communicate your impact more effectively.
- **Pathway Tags:** Academic Scholarship, Communications
- **Stage:** Developing an Idea, Finishing a Project
- **Format:** Immediate Tool
- **Time:** 40 minutes
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.25546/98474
- **Notes:** Individual-level impact mapping

---

## 5. Flows of Knowledge: A Method for Impact Assessment

- **Author:** Erasmus University Rotterdam
- **Purpose:** A framework for assessing non-academic impacts of research on policy, practice, and wider society. Informs readers about the method's building blocks and how it can assess the efficiency of knowledge exchange.
- **Value Proposition:** Need support developing a plan to assess the non-academic impact of your research? This tool offers a clear and simple framework for designing your own research impact assessment plan.
- **Pathway Tags:** Academic Scholarship, Policy
- **Stage:** Finishing a Project
- **Format:** Immediate Tool
- **Time:** 10 minutes
- **Link:** https://dataverse.nl/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.34894/ABLT4M
- **Notes:** Short, evaluation-focused. Spreadsheet notes: "Bring this framework into our impact planning module"

## 8. Thinking About my Impact and Engagement: A Worksheet

- **Author:** Erasmus University Rotterdam
- **Purpose:** A brief printable worksheet with accompanying presentation slides, designed for academics to consider how their project or individual work relates to society — impact goals, partners and beneficiaries, and engagement plans.
- **Value Proposition:** What does making positive societal impact mean to you? How do you map current and potential partners? This printable worksheet helps you align your work to their needs. Filling out the worksheet takes 15 minutes.
- **Pathway Tags:** Community Engagement
- **Stage:** Developing an Idea, Active Research
- **Format:** Immediate Tool
- **Time:** 20 minutes
- **Link:** https://dataverse.nl/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.34894/ZMU5F1
- **Notes:** Brief, printable — great for workshops and self-reflection

## 9. Theory of Change (ToC): A Method for Impact Planning and Assessment

- **Author:** Erasmus University Rotterdam
- **Purpose:** A tool for understanding when, where, and how to use the Theory of Change method in describing how you envision changes happening in a given context, or to specify outcomes and outputs of research activities.
- **Value Proposition:** Theory of Change is a formative, process-based evaluation method useful as a starting point for self-evaluation, grant application writing, strategy formulation, or mission statement drafting aiming for societal impact.
- **Pathway Tags:** Academic Scholarship
- **Stage:** Developing an Idea, Active Research
- **Format:** Immediate Tool
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Link:** https://dataverse.nl/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.34894/D0GMXS
- **Notes:** Core concept — fits well with impact planning module

- **Stage:** Developing an Idea
- **Format:** Immediate Tool
- **Time:** 16 minutes

## 11. Stakeholder Analysis Matrix Template

- **Author:** Tools4Dev
- **Purpose:** A practical template to help researchers and project teams systematically identify stakeholders, assess their influence and interest, and plan effective engagement strategies.
- **Value Proposition:** Map the people who make research matter. This template helps identify key partners, understand their influence and priorities, and plan how best to engage them throughout a research project.
- **Pathway Tags:** Community Engagement, Policy, Commercialization
- **Stage:** Developing an Idea
- **Format:** Immediate Tool
- **Time:** 3 minutes
- **Link:** https://tools4dev.org/resources/stakeholder-analysis-matrix-template/
- **Notes:** Quick, multi-pathway — lowest time commitment of all resources
