This information session supports researchers in writing effective op-eds that mobilize research knowledge in public discourse. Delivered by the Media Relations team, the session provides practical guidance on translating academic research into accessible commentary for general audiences.

Op-eds enable research to move beyond academic publication and enter public debate. They can amplify evidence, shape how issues are framed, and contribute to policy and societal discussions — strengthening the broader influence of your work.

## What you'll get

- How to draft a clear, focused op-ed
- How to write for non-specialist audiences
- How to connect research evidence to timely public issues
- Awareness of university media support and advisory resources
- Clearer direction for developing or refining an op-ed idea

## What to bring

- A defined research topic or idea
- Preliminary responses to the "5 Tips for Faculty" writing guidance
- Any draft text, if available

## Who it's for

Faculty members who want to engage public audiences through opinion media; contribute research-informed perspectives to current debates; increase the visibility and societal relevance of their work; or respond to emerging events through broader public engagement.

**Host:** University Communications Services — Media Relations Team, Concordia University. Available online or in-person (SGW campus). Two formats: group session (up to 10 participants) or one-on-one consultation. Contact Media Relations to schedule.
