This one-on-one consultation supports Concordia researchers with the review, negotiation, and administration of research agreements involving public and private sponsors. The service provides practical guidance on agreement terms, IP considerations, and institutional processes — helping researchers move smoothly from agreement to project completion.

Early attention to agreement terms and IP considerations helps reduce delays, manage risk, and support effective partnerships — enabling downstream research outcomes and impact.

## What you'll get

- Clarity on research agreement processes and institutional requirements
- Key IP considerations relevant to your project
- When and how to engage agreement support early
- Clear next steps for upcoming or active research agreements

## What to bring

- A short research summary, if available
- Any relevant information about upcoming or active agreements

## Who it's for

Researchers who are entering into research agreements with public or private sponsors; managing active research agreements; or anticipating upcoming agreements and wanting early IP or contractual guidance.

**Presenter:** Vanessa Buzzelli, Advisor, Internship and Service Agreements, PIPS — Office of Research, Concordia University. Online, 30-minute sessions. Recurring weekly, monthly, or quarterly — check LibCal for availability.
