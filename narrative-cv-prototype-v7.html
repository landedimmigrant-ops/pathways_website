Narrative CV

A drafting tool for your Tri-agency CV or CV-FRQ.

This tool helps you get familiar with how a narrative CV asks you to think about your work and describe it. You build a first outline in your own words: your contributions, your mentorship record and a personal statement. A full first draft can take a day or more, so allow time before your deadline. English only for now.

We recommend reading the narrative CV guide before you start, and consulting it as you go.
