Pathways to Impact · Concordia University

Research is where change begins.

This site brings together resources, services, and tools from across Concordia — curated by the Office of Research to support your research impact journey, wherever you are in it.

Defining research impact

What do we mean by change?

At Concordia, we define research impact as the change that research activities contribute to society and beyond — an ongoing process of engagement that can lead to academic, social, cultural, economic, environmental, or policy effects.

Learn more about research impact →

The challenge

The gap between knowledge and change is real.

Research doesn't automatically become impact. Knowledge moves through systems — academic, social, political — and the path is rarely straight. Most researchers navigate it without a map.

Plan your impact →

The framework

Seven pathways to impact.

Not a checklist — a starting place. Each pathway offers a different approach to creating real-world change from your research. Most researchers find more than one applies to them.

Explore the seven pathways →

The research lifecycle

Meet you where you are.

Impact support looks different at every stage. Pathways is designed for all three moments in your research project — developing an idea, active research, and finishing up.

Your support network

You don't navigate this alone.

Led by the Office of Research, Pathways to Impact brings together units from across the university — and beyond — to create a coordinated, growing support network around researchers. This is an evolving collaboration.
