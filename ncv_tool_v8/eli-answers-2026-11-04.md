# Eli's answers on four funder-rule lines (2026-11-04)

Prem's questions went to Eli on 2026-11-02 (draft in `email-to-eli-2026-11-02.md`). Eli's replies are summarised below, and each one says what changed in V8.6. The rule we follow is to apply Eli's answers as stated.

## 1. "Last five years" on the trainee totals
**Eli:** There is no time limit unless the funding opportunity specifically sets one. The researcher should check.
**V8.6:**
- The heading now reads "People you have trained" and the five-year wording is gone.
- The help under the boxes reads "These totals have no set period. Check the instructions for your funding opportunity in case it sets one."
- The draft, the Word file and the copied Mentorship section print "Totals:".
- The warning when more people are described than counted says "in your totals".

## 2. Where a career interruption goes
**Eli:**
- Check the eligibility criteria for early career.
- As Eli understands it, leaves go into the appendix, and researchers should check with their advisor.
- Someone with an extensive leave may still want to discuss it, possibly in the personal statement.
- Eli also suggested asking about leaves as part of the profile, and said "let's think" about it.

**V8.6:** The How did you get here help now:
- says leaves and other career interruptions usually go in the appendix of the application;
- asks researchers to check the instructions and eligibility criteria, especially as an early-career researcher, and to ask their research advisor;
- says they can mention a leave there, in one or two factual sentences, if it shaped their record and they want to discuss it.

The line under the totals no longer mentions leaves.

**INFERRED:**
- "the appendix of the application": Eli wrote "the appendix" without naming one.
- "your research advisor": Eli wrote "your advisor".

**Open (Eli: "let's think"):** whether the profile should ask about leaves. Nothing was built for this.

## 3. Links, DOIs and the FRQ sentence
**Eli:**
- SSHRC allows an audio-visual link when the work needs to be shown, and the tool should add this as evidence.
- Don't recommend DOIs, because researchers don't need them.
- There are no links, but a DOI number can go in as plain text.

**V8.6:**
- **New evidence kind:** "Audio or visual work", with its own hints (14 kinds).
- **SSHRC:** a link on an Audio or visual work row, or on an Exhibition, performance or screening row, is allowed and is not listed to take out. This works in any field; before 8.6 the exception covered creative fields only.
- **CIHR and NSERC:** the full no-links rule now applies, including to audio and visual work. Before 8.6 the tool said "where the funding opportunity allows it".
- **The TCV link rule in Format notes:** names the SSHRC exception and says a DOI can be written as plain text, without a link.
- **Link checks:** when a flagged link is a doi.org address, the check adds "A DOI can stay as plain text, without the link." A plain DOI such as `10.1234/abc` was never flagged and still is not.
- **No DOI prompts:** the tool still never suggests adding a DOI.

**Not answered:**
- whether the reason we proposed for the TCV's no-links rule is correct ("because reviewers read only the CV"), so the reason was not added;
- whether the FRQ sentence in the agency help is still accurate.
