## Message types (Prem, 2026-11-19)

Before writing a line, answer two questions: what are we telling the researcher, and what do we want them to do? Then pick the type. Each type has its own form, so a researcher can tell a tip from a requirement at a glance.

| Type | When | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instruction | Something the researcher must do to go on | One sentence: the goal, then the action. "Please" is fine. | "To Review and Export CV, please complete at least one contribution." |
| Hint | An optional tip that helps with the task | One sentence in quieter style, marked as a tip. Prem (2026-11-19) dropped the word "Hint:" and suggested an asterisk; Claude advised against the asterisk (it reads as "required" in forms, and as a footnote) and proposed a small info mark (a circled "i") with a hidden "Tip:" for screen readers, or the quieter style alone. Prem: "whatever will work with accessibility needs". Decided: the circled "i" (hidden from screen readers) plus a visually hidden "Tip:" that screen readers read, so the meaning is in words as well as in the mark; style alone would carry it by colour only (WCAG 1.4.1). Text at 4.5:1 contrast, the mark at 3:1. | "If a question is challenging, please consult your contributions." |
| Guidance | Orientation that helps the researcher understand the task (intros, the purpose of a step) | Short prose, two or three sentences, still plain words. Prem, 2026-11-19: "less tool function and more help and guidance". | S5 (Start) |
| Nothing | The line only describes what the screen already shows | Delete it | "You start with your contributions." (removed) |

Proposed, not yet agreed: a fourth type, **Funder rule**, for a requirement set by the agency (e.g. "These totals have no set period..."), and a fifth, **Status**, for what just happened ("Saved.").

Status values: **Logged** (noted, not decided) · **Open** (a question back to Prem) · **Decided** (wording agreed, not yet in the tool) · **Applied in V8.x** (in the tool).
