From the r8-b walk (a rehabilitation clinician-scientist writing in a third language, CIHR Project Grant). The persona read every label, prompt and help line and proposed plainer wording. Most of this text is Prem's or Eli's copy, so only the lines tied to a finding were changed this round. The rest is listed here to accept, edit or decline.

Mark each line **Yes**, **Edit: …** or **No** in the Decision column, and the next round applies it.
