The seven pathways

Explore the pathways below to find the lens that best fits your goals, audiences, and outputs.

Option 1 - Icon cards

Academic scholarship

Advance knowledge, methods, and scholarly contribution within and beyond your field.

Community engagement

Co-create research with communities and sustain reciprocal partnerships.

Innovation

Translate research into new methods, tools, or services that improve practice.

Commercialization

Move research-based ideas toward market-ready products or ventures.

Policy

Connect evidence to policy conversations and decision-making timelines.

Communications

Share research in accessible ways that reach public and professional audiences.

Research-creation

Create and present research through artistic and practice-based approaches.
