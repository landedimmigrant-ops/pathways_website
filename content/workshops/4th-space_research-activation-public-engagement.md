4th Space helps researchers turn their work into accessible, engaging public experiences. Through consultations, project development, event production, and follow-up support, this service provides the platform, team, and methods needed to connect with audiences and generate lasting impact.

This service addresses a common barrier to research impact: the gap between research production and meaningful public engagement. By supporting researchers to design, deliver, and document public-facing activations, 4th Space helps research become visible and usable beyond academic contexts.

## What you'll get

- How to design a public-facing event or activation tailored to your research
- How to connect with audiences in ways that support visibility, engagement, and impact
- How to develop shareable documentation that extends the life of your event
- How 4th Space can reduce the burden of planning, production, and technical coordination
- How to think about media, social platforms, and archival documentation as part of an impact strategy

## Service stages

1. **Initial Inquiry** — 60-minute consultation with the 4th Space Team Lead
2. **Project Development** — Planning meeting with lead, coordinator, and technical specialist
3. **Event Production** — Public activation in the 4th Space or hybrid format
4. **Follow-Up & Impact Tracking** — Documentation, analytics, and publishing options

## What to bring

- Physical materials or objects from your research
- A clear sense of your project's stage and audience goals
- A current challenge or opportunity you want to address
- A sense of your target audiences

## Who it's for

Researchers who want to make their projects public, engage communities, test ideas in front of audiences, or capture documentation that supports long-term impact and reporting.

**Host:** 4th Space, Concordia University (SGW campus). Off-site visits and hybrid delivery available.
