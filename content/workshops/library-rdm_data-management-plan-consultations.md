Preparing a Data Management Plan for a grant or award? This one-on-one consultation with a librarian helps you answer your specific DMP questions, understand the DMP Assistant tool, and choose templates aligned with your research data.

Good data management strengthens impact by enabling research outputs to be preserved, shared, and taken up beyond the life of a grant — supporting open science and long-term research reuse.

## What you'll get

- How to structure a DMP that meets funder requirements
- How to use the DMP Assistant tool effectively
- Which DMP templates best fit your research data
- How to address storage, documentation, ethics, access, retention, and preservation
- How good data management supports long-term impact, sharing, and reuse

## What to bring

Please share the following at least one week before your appointment:

- A draft DMP and/or a detailed research description with the type of data to be collected
- Specific questions you would like to address

## Who it's for

Researchers and research teams preparing funding applications that require a Data Management Plan, or those seeking guidance on managing their research data.

**Host:** Concordia University Library — Research Data Management (RDM) Team. Online delivery, all campuses welcome.
