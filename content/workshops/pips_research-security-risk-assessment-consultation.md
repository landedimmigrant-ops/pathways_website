This one-on-one consultation introduces researchers to how risk-based assessments of external research partnerships and sensitive research areas are conducted. The session walks through the requirements, the types of risks reviewed, and explains the steps involved in a follow-up assessment.

Responsible research depends on trust, integrity, and well-governed partnerships. Early identification and mitigation of risks helps protect research integrity, maintain viable collaborations, and ensure alignment with funder and regulatory requirements.

## What you'll get

- How to identify and assess risks linked to external partnerships and sensitive research areas
- Clarity on security, reputational, and compliance considerations relevant to your project
- Tailored mitigation strategies appropriate to your research context
- Next steps for implementing safeguards across the research lifecycle
- Greater confidence in meeting funder and regulatory expectations

## What to bring

- A short project or partnership summary, if available
- Any relevant information about external collaborators or funder requirements

## Who it's for

Researchers working with external partners in Canada or internationally; engaged in research areas with heightened security or compliance considerations; navigating funder or sanctions-related requirements; or unsure how to assess or manage partnership-related risks.
