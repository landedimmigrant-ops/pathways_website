This one-on-one consultation supports Concordia researchers who are exploring how to protect and translate innovations into real-world applications. An IP specialist from the technology transfer unit provides practical, case-specific guidance on invention disclosure, IP strategy, and available pathways for advancing research beyond the university.

Making informed decisions about disclosure, protection, and commercialization can enable licensing, new partnerships, startup creation, and other forms of public implementation.

## What you'll get

- The stages of the technology transfer process: research → invention disclosure → evaluation → IP protection → implementation pathway
- Timing considerations related to publication and patentability, and risks associated with public disclosure
- Tailored guidance relevant to your specific case
- Clear next steps to move forward in the technology transfer process

## What to bring

- Readiness to discuss your innovation and its current stage
- Any relevant documents, if available (e.g., draft manuscript, invention disclosure materials, agreements, or policies)

## Who it's for

Researchers working on inventions, tools, methods, software, or technologies with potential real-world application; those preparing to publish or share results; those considering patenting, licensing, or building industry partnerships; or those exploring spin-off or startup pathways.

**Presenter:** Valeria Marquet, Senior IP Specialist, Technology Transfer Unit, PIPS — Office of Research, Concordia University. Online, 15-minute appointment blocks. Book based on available slots.
