# Pathways Lexicon

Word-level rulings for Pathways copy. Companion to `.claude/skills/pathways-voice/`.
Evidence: `register-corpus.md`. Verified 2026-08-10.

Three columns of judgement: **Use** (house-preferred), **Careful** (fine in the right register or
with a gloss), **Avoid** (reaches for the podium, or is simply wrong).

---

## 1. Funder vocabulary — get these exactly right

Wrong funder terms are a **correctness** failure. See `reference.md` §1 for sources.

| Use | Not | Note |
|---|---|---|
| knowledge mobilization (KMb / KM) | knowledge transfer | SSHRC and CIHR now share this umbrella term |
| research co-production | integrated knowledge translation, iKT | CIHR retired iKT in 2026 |
| knowledge translation (KT) | — | **Historical reference only.** Not a live CIHR offer |
| knowledge user | stakeholder, end user, target audience | CIHR's definition is specific — use it when you mean it |
| knowledge holder | — | Traditional or cultural knowledge. CIHR credits Concordia for this definition |
| research-creation | practice-based research, arts research | SSHRC's defined term. Concordia pairs "research or research-creation" |
| emerging scholar / early-career researcher | junior researcher, young researcher | *Young* is an age claim you don't mean |
| highly qualified personnel (HQP) | — | Correct inside Tri-agency CV context; gloss elsewhere |
| people living in Canada | Canadians | Avoids conflating residency with citizenship |
| open science / open scholarship | open research | Concordia uses both, paired |
| knowledge synthesis | literature review | Different activity |
| knowledge brokering | networking | — |
| Indigenous Peoples | — | Both words capitalized in federal program text |
| women and gender-equity seeking groups, persons with disabilities, Indigenous Peoples and racialized minorities | visible minorities | CRC's own wording. *Visible minority* belongs only to *Employment Equity Act* contexts |

## 2. Verbs

Performability (V3): can the reader start this today?

> **Where verb-first belongs.** The corpus measures imperative sentences at 3–8% across every
> source, Concordia and agencies alike. Verb-first phrasing is for **headings, buttons and list
> items**. Running instructional prose is *second person + modal* — see §2b. A page written entirely
> in imperatives sounds like neither Concordia nor a funder.

| Use — performable | Avoid — outcomes in imperative costume |
|---|---|
| read, write, draft, revise, rewrite | leverage, maximize, optimize |
| find, choose, pick, compare | enhance, elevate, amplify |
| book, attend, join, contact, ask | empower, unlock, unleash |
| map, list, name, number, tag | drive, foster, cultivate, spearhead |
| gather, collect, record, cite | utilize *(just say "use")* |
| check, test, review, audit | facilitate *(name what you actually do)* |
| plan, schedule, set aside time | strengthen *(strengthen how?)* |
| show, explain, describe | showcase, highlight *(usually padding)* |
| start, finish, submit | embark, undertake |

**"Support" is the project's occupational hazard.** It is the right noun for what Pathways offers
and the wrong verb for almost every sentence. "Connect with support for evaluation" names nothing.
"Book an hour with an evaluation advisor" names an action, a duration and a person.

### 2b. Modals — the obligation ladder

The single most important word class in instructional copy, and the easiest to get factually wrong.

| Modal | Use for | Concordia rate | Note |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **must** | A genuine funder or policy requirement | 0.6/1k | NSERC runs 1.8/1k. Relay at the funder's own strength |
| **need to** | A practical necessity for the outcome | 0.6/1k | Softer than *must*, still binding in practice |
| **should** | Pathways' own advice | 0.8/1k | Concordia is the **only** source in the corpus that meaningfully uses this |
| **can** | An option or a capability | 1.1/1k | The grammar of possibility — Concordia leads on it |
| **may** | Genuine permission or possibility | — | Don't use it to hedge a requirement |

**Never downgrade a funder's modal.** If an agency says *must*, Pathways says *must* and attributes
it. Advisory voice is house style for our own guidance, not for relaying rules.

### 2c. Politeness

*Please* runs **4.4 per 1,000 words** on Concordia instruction pages — against 0.2 at SSHRC and 0.6
at NSERC. It is not padding here; it is house style, and it is what makes an instruction read as a
request from a colleague rather than a condition of funding. Keep it in support moves ("please
contact…") and out of hard requirements ("you must" needs no softening).

## 3. Nouns and abstractions

| Careful | Why |
|---|---|
| impact | The project's name. Never a bare modifier — "impact language", "impact work", "impact activities" are usually padding around a missing noun |
| support | Fine as a noun for the offer; weak as a verb (§2) |
| ecosystem, landscape, space | Metaphors that have stopped meaning anything. Name the actual thing |
| journey | Tolerable for research *stages*; grating everywhere else |
| solution | Almost always a vaguer word than the one you meant |
| stakeholder | Imprecise. If you mean someone who can act on the research, say **knowledge user** |
| resources | Fine in a nav label; weak in prose — say what kind |

| Avoid outright | Instead |
|---|---|
| seamless, integrated, holistic, robust, comprehensive | Delete. If deleting changes no promise, it promised nothing |
| cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, innovative | Show the thing; let the reader conclude it |
| synergy, alignment *(as a noun)*, touchpoint, deliverable | Say what happens and who does it |
| curated | Say who chose it and on what basis |
| game-changing, transformative *(loose use)* | Reserve *transformative* for where a funder uses it as a criterion |

## 4. Mechanics (WIRE / Canadian Press)

| Rule | Right | Wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Canadian *-our* | colour, favour, rigour, behaviour, honour | color, behavior |
| Canadian *-re* | centre, metre, theatre | center, meter |
| but *-ize / -yze* | analyze, organize, recognize, mobilize | analyse, organise |
| single *-l* words | enrol, fulfil *(but* enrolled, fulfilling*)* | enroll, fulfill |
| program | program | programme |
| **No Oxford comma** | "tools, guidance and training" | "tools, guidance, and training" |
| Numbers | zero–nine spelled; 10+ numerals | "9 pathways" |
| Numbers at sentence start | "Seven pathways organize…" | "7 pathways organize…" |
| Percentages | per cent | % *(in prose)* |
| Capitalize | Indigenous, Black | indigenous, black |
| Titles before a name | "President Graham Carr" | "president Graham Carr" |
| Titles after a name | "Graham Carr, president" | "Graham Carr, President" |
| Decades | the 1990s, the '90s | the 1990's |
| Em dash | spaced en dash or em dash, used consistently | mixing both in one document |

### Two exceptions that override everything above

1. **Direct quotations** — quote as written. CIHR's glossary says "behaviors"; leave it.
2. **Proper nouns** — *Erasmus Medical Center*, *4th Space*, *District 3* keep their own spelling
   and capitalization.

## 5. Concordia-specific

| Item | Ruling |
|---|---|
| research **or** research-creation | Concordia pairs these deliberately. Keep the pairing where both are meant — dropping it silently excludes Fine Arts |
| Office of the Vice-President, Research, Innovation and Impact | Spell out on first use, then **OVPRII** |
| Pathways to Impact | Full name on first use per page; *Pathways* thereafter is acceptable in DESK copy |
| Tri-agency / Tri-council | **Tri-agency** for the funders acting jointly (their own term). *Tri-council* is common speech but not the agencies' current usage |
| FRQ | Fonds de recherche du Québec — spell out first, keep the accent |
| Québec | Accent in the provincial-government and FRQ context; *Quebec* in general CP prose. Be consistent within a document |
| impact@concordia.ca | The contact address. Never invent an alternate |
| the university | Lowercase in running prose |

## 6. Words that signal the wrong register instantly

If any of these survive a DESK pass, the pass didn't happen:

> leverage · seamless · robust · holistic · synergy · cutting-edge · world-class · empower ·
> unlock · curated · best-in-class · thought leadership · deep dive · move the needle ·
> at the end of the day · in today's rapidly evolving landscape

## 7. Things that sound like errors but aren't

- **Long qualified sentences in guidance copy.** CP's brevity rule is a wire-service constraint. A
  27-word sentence that carries a necessary qualification is better writing than two short ones that
  drop it.
- **Contractions.** "Don't", "you're", "we're" are in Concordia's own published researcher-facing
  copy. They belong in DESK.
- **Admitting uncertainty.** "We don't have an answer for this question yet" is published Concordia
  copy and the strongest trust move in the corpus.
- **Agency acronyms.** KMb, HQP, EDI, DORA, TCV are not jargon to this audience. Gloss once, then use.
