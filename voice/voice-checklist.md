# Pathways Voice — Pre-Publish Checklist

One page. Run before any Pathways copy ships — site strings, guides, NCV tool text, emails, decks.
Full system: `.claude/skills/pathways-voice/SKILL.md`.

---

## Step 1 — Name the register

- [ ] **Who reads this?** A researcher deciding what to do → **DESK**.
      A provost, funder, board or partner institution → **PODIUM**.
- [ ] Default is **DESK**. If you chose PODIUM, write down why.
- [ ] Home hero and other mixed surfaces: DESK, plus **one** PODIUM sentence maximum.

---

## Step 2 — Voice checks

**T1 · The Tuesday Test** — read each instruction and ask: *could someone start this Tuesday morning?*
- [ ] No head verbs from the kill list: leverage, maximize, enhance, elevate, unlock, empower,
      utilize, drive, foster, strengthen, showcase.
- [ ] Verb-first is not enough. "Maximize your impact" is a command that names no action.

**T2 · The Subject Test** — look at what each sentence is *about*.
- [ ] The grammatical subject is a person or a concrete thing, not *collaboration*, *coordination*,
      *support*, or *the initiative*.
- [ ] No agentless passives hiding who is responsible.

**T3 · The Evidence Test**
- [ ] Every claim about impact names its evidence, or tells the reader where to get it.
- [ ] No adjectives doing an evidence's job (*credible*, *meaningful*, *far-reaching*).

---

## Step 3 — Correctness checks (these make copy *wrong*)

**T4 · Term Test**
- [ ] Funder terms are exact — no invented friendly synonyms. Check `lexicon.md` §1.
- [ ] **knowledge mobilization**, not knowledge transfer.
- [ ] **research co-production**, not integrated knowledge translation / iKT *(retired 2026)*.
- [ ] *Knowledge translation (KT)* appears only as history, never as a live offer.
- [ ] Each agency term glossed once on first use, then used plainly.

**T5 · Metric Test**
- [ ] Nothing encourages **h-index**, **career-total citations**, or **Journal Impact Factor**.
- [ ] Individual-article citation counts appear only in support of a qualitative claim.

---

## Step 4 — Mechanics (Canadian Press)

- [ ] Canadian spelling: colour, behaviour, rigour, centre, analyze, enrol, program.
- [ ] **No Oxford comma** — "tools, guidance and training".
- [ ] Numbers: zero–nine spelled out, 10 and up as numerals; spelled out at sentence start.
- [ ] *per cent*, not % in prose.
- [ ] **Indigenous** and **Black** capitalized.
- [ ] Titles capitalized before a name only.
- [ ] Dashes and quotation marks consistent throughout the document.

**Do not "fix" these two:**
- [ ] Direct quotations — quote as written, even when the spelling is American.
- [ ] Proper nouns — *Erasmus Medical Center*, *4th Space*, *District 3*.

---

## Step 5 — Final sweep

**T7 · Density** — [ ] "impact" is not used as a bare modifier ("impact language", "impact work").

**T8 · Futurity** — [ ] No undated "launching soon" or "coming soon". Date it or cut it.

- [ ] Read the whole thing aloud. Anything you'd never say to a colleague comes out.
- [ ] Every "support" that could be a specific action is a specific action.
- [ ] `research or research-creation` pairing kept wherever both are meant.

---

## When checks disagree

1. T4 term accuracy and T5 metrics — they make copy wrong. They win.
2. Quotations and proper nouns — never edited for house style.
3. Mechanics (CP).
4. Voice (T1–T3).
5. Density and futurity — cosmetic. Drop them if a fix costs clarity.

**A long sentence carrying a real qualification beats a short one that drops it.**

---

## Reference sentence

When a rewrite isn't landing, compare against Concordia's own best published line:

> "Allow time: it can take a day or more to get your first draft ready."

Performable verb. Second person. Honest number. No adjective doing work a fact should do.
