# Pathways Voice — Pre-Publish Checklist

One page. Run before any Pathways copy ships — site strings, guides, NCV tool text, emails, decks.
Full system: `.claude/skills/pathways-voice/SKILL.md`. Evidence: `construction-study.md`.

**The reader is always a researcher.** Every block does one of three jobs.

---

## Step 0 — Name the job

- [ ] Mark each block: **communicate** (understand) · **instruct** (act) · **support** (get unstuck).
- [ ] A page usually runs through all three, in that order. If it never reaches *instruct*, ask why
      it exists.

---

## Step 1 — COMMUNICATE

**V1 · Reader-first**
- [ ] The opening sentence names **what the reader gets**, not what Pathways is.
- [ ] Good: "This page will help you…" / "Get familiar with… so you can…"
- [ ] Bad: any opener whose subject is Concordia, Pathways, or an initiative.

**V2 · Address**
- [ ] Second person throughout. Target **15–20 "you" per 1,000 words**; keep "we" under 4.
- [ ] Quick gauge: if "we" outnumbers "you" anywhere on the page, it's a brochure.

**Headings**
- [ ] On task pages, **25–40% of headings are questions** — in the researcher's own voice, first
      person. "I think I've invented something. What now?" not "Invention disclosure."
- [ ] Noun headings only for navigation and directories, where the reader already knows the term.

---

## Step 2 — INSTRUCT

**V3 · Performability**
- [ ] Every instruction names an action the reader can start, with a **specific object** — the form,
      the system, the team, the document.
- [ ] No head verbs from the kill list: leverage, maximize, enhance, elevate, unlock, empower,
      utilize, foster, strengthen, showcase.

**V4 · Condition-first**
- [ ] If it only applies sometimes, the condition comes **first**: "If you… / To… / For…"
- [ ] The reader can skip the sentence without reading to the end.

**V5 · Modal accuracy**
- [ ] *must* only for a genuine funder or policy requirement — at the funder's own strength.
- [ ] *should* / *can* for Pathways' own advice.
- [ ] **Softening an agency's "must" into "should" is a factual error, not a tone choice.**

**V6 · Time anchor**
- [ ] Every instruction with a deadline, lead time or sequence says so: "at least 10 working days
      ahead", "within 2 working days", "before you submit".

**Prose, not commands**
- [ ] Running instruction is **second person + modal**, not imperative. Imperatives belong in
      headings, buttons and list items.
- [ ] Lists use the label construction: **Handle** — imperative. Reason.

---

## Step 3 — SUPPORT

**V7 · Named handoff**
- [ ] A **named person with a title**, or a named team — not "the appropriate office".
- [ ] The channel is stated (email, form, phone).
- [ ] What the helper will actually do is listed: "can help prepare the agreement and take care of
      the transfer", not "can support you".
- [ ] There is a failure catch: "If you cannot find what you need, don't hesitate to…"
- [ ] Where we genuinely don't know, we say so.

---

## Step 4 — ALWAYS

**V8 · Funder facts**
- [ ] Agency terms exact: **knowledge mobilization** (not knowledge transfer), **research
      co-production** (not integrated knowledge translation / iKT — retired 2026).
- [ ] *Knowledge translation (KT)* appears only as history, never as a live offer.
- [ ] Nothing encourages **h-index**, **career-total citations**, or **Journal Impact Factor**.

**V9 · Mechanics (Canadian Press)**
- [ ] Canadian spelling: colour, behaviour, rigour, centre, analyze, enrol, program.
- [ ] **No Oxford comma.**
- [ ] Numbers: zero–nine spelled out, 10+ as numerals; spelled out at sentence start.
- [ ] *per cent*, not % in prose.
- [ ] **Indigenous** and **Black** capitalized.
- [ ] Do **not** "fix" direct quotations or proper nouns (*Erasmus Medical Center* stays).

---

## Step 5 — Length

Instruction is the **longest** thing Concordia writes. Don't cut the condition to hit a word count.

- [ ] Sentences 15–25 words.
- [ ] Paragraphs under 35 words.
- [ ] List items under 20 words. (CIHR's 35-word bullets are the failure case.)

---

## When checks disagree

1. **V5 modals** and **V8 funder facts** — they make copy wrong. They win.
2. Quotations and proper nouns — never edited for house style.
3. **V9** mechanics.
4. **V1–V4, V6, V7** — voice and construction.
5. Cosmetics — drop them if a fix costs clarity.

---

## Reference sentences

The three best sentences in the corpus, one per job:

> **Communicate** — "This page will help guide you through everything you need to know about
> managing your grant."
>
> **Instruct** — "If you believe that you have developed an invention with commercial potential, you
> need to submit a Declaration of Invention (DOI) form to the Office of Research."
>
> **Support** — "For developmental content review and feedback, please contact your Advisor,
> Research Development directly by email at least 10 working days ahead of the agency deadlines."

Situation, reader, obligation, specific destination, timing. Nothing decorative.
